basalt
An Oculin village at dusk beneath a mountain ridge. Architecture distinctly alien: tall curving black basalt teeth, hollowed chambers inside them, oval eye-shaped slit openings lit with milky blue lamps, suspended stone chimes hung under the stone lips. No pitched roof, timber cottage, castle, humans or European village. Near one open dwelling an Oculin parent teaches a child to thread a pebble onto a hanging chime; behind them villagers gather supper in ceramic bowls. A beloved everyday home to these compact slate grey single-violet-eyed, two blunt horned cyclopes. Peaceful interior life, ominous silhouette. The same chimes recur later as a symbol.
