Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one exploratory character concept for the evil god embodied by the player in a fantasy dungeon-building game. The attached old cyclops shaman is ONLY a reference for the softly painted game-art rendering, tactile materials and level of simplification. This new subject is the god worshipped by that shaman, NOT an Oculin, not a variant of him, not a cyclops in a hood. Design an ancient, imposing, ominous, intelligent presence with strong readable silhouette and genuine dimensional form. Match the reference's stylized hand-painted game asset feel, soft edges, matte surfaces, readable large shapes; no photorealism, no tiny noisy ornament, no heavy black cartoon outlines. A tangible design that can later be modeled in 3D, restrained violet supernatural light rather than a cloud hiding the design. Single full-body character, three-quarter front view, completely in frame with 8 percent margins, slightly elevated camera, neutral presentation pose, hands empty. One figure only, centered in square canvas. Genuine transparent alpha background, no environment, no floor plane, no cast shadow, no labels, no text, no reference shaman, no weapon. This is one design proposal, not a multi-view sheet. A squat monumental god of black basalt and dark mineral stone. Extremely broad shoulders, very long massive forearms and short powerful pillar-like legs with large distinct feet. Several enormous asymmetrical natural rock masses form a coherent sculptural body; organic geological fractures NOT masonry bricks. Narrow head recessed between the shoulders; an eroded angular stone crown is integral to its skull. A deep small pair of violet eyes underneath its brow; no big cyclops eye or familiar human face. A diamond-shaped cavity of subdued violet light inside the torso, hairline violet cracks across only a few rocks. Ancient worn bronze binding bands sparingly around wrists and chest. No robe, cloak, smoke skirt or armor suit. Dominant silhouette wide, grounded and monolithic.
