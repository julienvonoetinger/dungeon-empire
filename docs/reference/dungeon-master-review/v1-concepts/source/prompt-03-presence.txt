Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one exploratory character concept for the evil god embodied by the player in a fantasy dungeon-building game. The attached old cyclops shaman is ONLY a reference for the softly painted game-art rendering, tactile materials and level of simplification. This new subject is the god worshipped by that shaman, NOT an Oculin, not a variant of him, not a cyclops in a hood. Design an ancient, imposing, ominous, intelligent presence with strong readable silhouette and genuine dimensional form. Match the reference's stylized hand-painted game asset feel, soft edges, matte surfaces, readable large shapes; no photorealism, no tiny noisy ornament, no heavy black cartoon outlines. A tangible design that can later be modeled in 3D, restrained violet supernatural light rather than a cloud hiding the design. Single full-body character, three-quarter front view, completely in frame with 8 percent margins, slightly elevated camera, neutral presentation pose, hands empty. One figure only, centered in square canvas. Genuine transparent alpha background, no environment, no floor plane, no cast shadow, no labels, no text, no reference shaman, no weapon. This is one design proposal, not a multi-view sheet. An enigmatic nonhuman manifested god. Floating and elongated, a smooth ancient obsidian oval mask with a single thin VERTICAL violet fissure (not an eye), crowned by three broken stone arches hovering close behind it. Narrow solid dark torso with a few aged bronze bands, very long sculptural arms ending in distinct elegant four-clawed hands held low. Lower body narrows into two intertwined solid tapered dark tendrils like twisted draped stone, surrounded by only a little wispy violet shadow. A volumetric 3D body with visible side planes, NOT a flat smoke sticker. No hood, no robe, no human face, no conventional legs or armored boots. Restrained mysterious presence, poised rather than attacking. Dominant silhouette elongated, floating, tapering and slightly asymmetrical. Most form is solid and readable; wisps occupy under ten percent of the silhouette.
