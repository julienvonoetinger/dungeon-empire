Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: one exploratory character concept for the evil god embodied by the player in a fantasy dungeon-building game. The attached old cyclops shaman is ONLY a reference for the softly painted game-art rendering, tactile materials and level of simplification. This new subject is the god worshipped by that shaman, NOT an Oculin, not a variant of him, not a cyclops in a hood. Design an ancient, imposing, ominous, intelligent presence with strong readable silhouette and genuine dimensional form. Match the reference's stylized hand-painted game asset feel, soft edges, matte surfaces, readable large shapes; no photorealism, no tiny noisy ornament, no heavy black cartoon outlines. A tangible design that can later be modeled in 3D, restrained violet supernatural light rather than a cloud hiding the design. Single full-body character, three-quarter front view, completely in frame with 8 percent margins, slightly elevated camera, neutral presentation pose, hands empty. One figure only, centered in square canvas. Genuine transparent alpha background, no environment, no floor plane, no cast shadow, no labels, no text, no reference shaman, no weapon. This is one design proposal, not a multi-view sheet. An ancient organic nonhuman mountain god, a tall hunchbacked imposing creature with very wide muscular upper back, two powerful long arms, thick clawed hands, digitigrade legs and two visible broad three-toed feet. Weathered deep umber hide with large bone-colored mineral plates grown organically along shoulders and forearms. Head looks like a compact faceless animal-shaped stone skull with two swept-back branching horn roots, a low violet glow within two deep narrow sockets, no human nose or smile, no hair or beard. A few coarse plum ritual bindings and small weathered bronze clasps, no clothes covering the body and no armor suit. An ancient protective but dangerous creature whose power feels older than kingdoms. No gore, exposed flesh, wings, weapons or staff. Dominant silhouette primal, bent, muscular and organic, clearly distinct from a rock golem or human armored king.
