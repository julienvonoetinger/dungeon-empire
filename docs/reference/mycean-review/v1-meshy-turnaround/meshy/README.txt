Use the front as the primary image; left, back and right are the three
complementary Multi-View images. Upload these separate PNGs, not the review
page or a contact sheet. All five PNGs are1254x1254 with native transparency.

Keep the neutral A pose and empty hands for rigging. The mushroom cap belongs
to the character, not a separate hat. Preserve the cream stem-face, broad violet
cap with cyan spots, green/violet leaf clothing, brown boots and leather harness.
There are TWO small mushrooms on the character's RIGHT rear shoulder and ONE
pouch on the RIGHT hip (imageLEFT in front, imageRIGHT in back). The pouch's
back is plain leather; its flap and clasp appear only on its front/outer side.
Do not add a second pouch, long cape, beard, hair or staff to the body.

EQUIPMENT — SEPARATE GENERATION
equipment/staff.png is one continuous fungal staff: its twisted woody stalk
flows directly into the gills and blue-violet cap. It is NOT a little mushroom
perched in a branch fork. Keep the central grip and root end. Generate as a separate
object. Do NOT put this image in a character Multi-View slot: it is not another
view of the body. The staff can later be attached to a hand in Blender.

Export a textured rigged GLB of the character, as for the previous heroes.
Export the staff as a separate textured GLB. Actual sprite views will be rendered
from the resulting model with the game's isometric camera, not these side views.

These are independent AI illustrations, not calibrated renders of an existing
mesh. Small shape/painting differences remain. Reconstruction and rigging in
Meshy have not been tested. All PNGs preserve original generated bytes, without
resizing, flipping or scripted painting. Source prompts and provenance are
archived alongside this pack in the repository.
