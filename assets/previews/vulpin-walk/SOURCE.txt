Four PNG sheets copied unchanged from:
docs/reference/vulpin-review/v16-meshy-walk/sprites/

8 held poses per direction, 256 x 256 per frame, authored at 10 FPS.
Normal preview playback is now 15 FPS (the former 1.5x), with matching travel.
Original model: user-supplied Meshy_AI_Character_output.glb.
Sprite production and packed Blender source remain in the v16 review folder.
No 3D model is required by this scene.

Open VulpinWalk.tscn in Godot and press F6.
Two native three-cell corridors show SE/NW and SW/NE round trips.
Translation and the eight rendered poses share a single playback clock.
The initial travel rate follows the planted-foot stride of the Blender poses.
Space: pause/resume. Right arrow: pause and advance one pose.
Buttons expose pause, stepping (including travel), restart and 0.5x/1x/1.5x/2x speed.
These multipliers are relative to the new normal speed.

These sprites are a visual trial and are not assigned to gameplay heroes.
