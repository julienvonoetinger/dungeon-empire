# Art Direction Constraints

## Core direction
**Supernatural · Readable · Stylized**

The game uses stylized dark fantasy with a supernatural dungeon identity and strong readability at mobile scale.

## Canonical rendering rules
- stylized low-poly 3D, hand-painted/PBR-inspired materials, readable silhouette, not chibi.
- single isolated asset, full object visible, neutral 3/4 or isometric view suitable for image-to-3D.
- plain dark neutral background; no scenery, no grid, no text, no labels.
- Dungeon materials: anthracite and blue-gray stone, restrained dark metal, limited violet supernatural energy.
- Supernatural energy: deep violet to magenta emissive accents; do not make the entire object purple.
- Wealth: warm gold / brass.
- Hero proportions: roughly 5–6 heads tall; exaggerated for readability but not chibi.
- The Dungeon Master is never directly shown. Its presence is expressed through transformed rock, energy veins, Core pulses and spatial distortion.
- The Core is a suspended **dark spatial anomaly / void**, not a generic crystal.

## Avoid
- photorealism
- excessive gore
- generic red demon/lava/pentagram look
- Dungeon Keeper imitation
- overly comedic chibi proportions
- contact sheets or multi-object compositions
- text, filenames, labels or UI in the image
- bird beaks on insectoid designs

## Species
Preferred surface species include Vulpins, Batrafians, Lithides, Nocturnes, Myceans, Saurians, Forged beings and a minority of Humans.

**Insectoid is excluded from the current production manifest.** Earlier concepts visually drifted toward a bird-like beak and are not canonical.
